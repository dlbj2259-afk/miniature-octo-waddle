A 60-second look at the evidence

Were people tricked into the COVID vaccine?

Lots of people believe it. Here's what the data actually shows, including the parts officials got wrong.

01 · What's in the shot

A recipe for one protein, wrapped in fat

Your cell reads the mRNA and builds the virus's spike protein, nothing else. The mRNA never enters the nucleus and has no way to rewrite DNA.

02 · Training, not infection

The immune system studies the spike

Antibodies and memory cells learn the spike's shape. If the real virus shows up later, the body recognises it faster and fights harder.

03 · "It was rushed"

Fast, but built on 30 years of work

The speed came from overlapping trial phases and governments paying for manufacturing before results were in. The safety-testing phases still happened.

04 · The Pfizer trial · 43,548 people

Who got sick with COVID?

Roughly 21,700 people in each arm. Each dot is one confirmed case. Moderna's trial of 30,000 found 94%. Polack et al., NEJM 2020 · Baden et al., NEJM 2021

05 · The real side effects

Rare risks existed, and monitoring caught them

Most myocarditis cases were mild and resolved. COVID infection itself also causes myocarditis. These risks were found and published, not hidden. Oster et al., JAMA 2022 · CDC/FDA, 2021–22

06 · Where the messaging went wrong

"Stops the spread" was overpromised

Protection against catching COVID faded within months, especially against Omicron. Protection against severe illness held up much better. Officials sounded more certain than the data, and that cost trust. Andrews et al., NEJM 2022 · Lin et al., NEJM 2022

07 · The verdict
~14.4 million

deaths estimated prevented worldwide in the vaccines' first year.Watson et al., Lancet Infectious Diseases 2022 (modelling study)

Not a trick. A tested tool with real, rare risks, and public messaging that should have been more honest about its limits.
