## 3. Goals & KPIs (proposed, 🔎)
- Primary KPI: **shares per 1,000 views**. For a creator still growing an audience, shares are what push a video to new viewers. It's TikTok's strongest signal, and shares and saves matter most on IG and FB.
- Supporting: **average watch % / completion rate** (does the hook and pacing hold?), and **follows per 1,000 views** (do viewers turn into followers?).
- Vanity (report, don't optimize): likes, total views on their own.
- Cadence: a weekly 28-day review; a monthly 90-day review.

## 5. Voice (🔎, to confirm against real posts)
- Curious, but not clickbait. Stats-backed, but not dry. Cinematic, but not slow. Confident, but not preachy.
- Signature moves: a "What if…" question hook, a running counter or time-lapse, word-by-word captions, a "The science" outro that cites sources.
- Never say: made-up numbers presented as fact. Label hypotheticals, as the boom video does.
